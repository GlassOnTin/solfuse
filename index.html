Recovered from a limb in your own stack — a step edge of known geometry, sampled all the way around the arc. Nothing about its shape is assumed, so defocus, seeing and scattering each look like what they are.

PSF
radial profile (log)

Richardson–Lucy with the measured PSF, plus two constraints the geometry gives for free: regions known to be dark are held there, and clipped pixels are excluded because they only bound the truth from below. Fewer iterations is better than more. Against synthetic ground truth the optimum is 2 iterations — RMSE 1.43 against 2.64 for the blurred input, a 1.85× improvement, with fine-band correlation to the truth rising from 0.970 to 0.977. Three is slightly worse, five is worse than not deconvolving at all, and twenty is catastrophic. Deconvolution is fundamentally ill-posed: the PSF's response near Nyquist is so small that even rounding error gets amplified without limit, so the useful range is narrow.

Wavelet sharpening, the à trous scheme. Each slider scales one band of detail; at 1.00 the image is reconstructed exactly. Sensor and codec noise live mostly in the ~1 px band, so lifting ~2 and ~4 px while holding ~1 px at or below 1.00 sharpens structure without amplifying grain. Re-renders from the finished stack, so it is instant.
